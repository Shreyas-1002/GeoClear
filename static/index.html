GeoClear: Multimodal Satellite Cloud Removal

An open, data-aware Earth Observation AI platform designed to reconstruct cloud-occluded multispectral surface reflectance by fusing active Copernicus Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) with Sentinel-2 Optical (MSI) imagery.

🛰️ Sentinel-1 C-SAR (5.405 GHz) 🌈 Sentinel-2 MSI (13 Spectral Bands) ⚡ Real CPU Inference 📐 10m Pixel Resolution 🔬 Zero-Fabrication Metrics Policy
The Cloud Occlusion Challenge

Persistent cloud contamination contaminates over 67% of global Earth surface satellite imagery at any moment. During monsoon and tropical seasons, cloud cover exceeds 80–90%, blinding optical instruments for weeks or months.

Traditional optical compositing fails during prolonged overcast periods, while pure GAN inpainting hallucinates plausible textures without physical basis. GeoClear overcomes this by incorporating cloud-penetrating radar.

Multimodal SAR-Optical Fusion

Sentinel-1 C-band SAR (5.405 GHz, λ ≈ 5.55 cm) passes unaffected through atmospheric clouds, rain, and haze. The backscatter signals (σ0 in dB for VV and VH polarizations) capture surface roughness, moisture, and urban double-bounce reflections.

By coupling SAR geometric structural priors with residual optical albedo, our deep learning architectures reconstruct all 13 multispectral bands without geometric hallucinations.

Detailed Per-Band Errors (MAE & RMSE)

Band Spectral Channel Wavelength MAE RMSE
Investigation of Low Band 10 MAE: Band 10 (1375 nm) is located inside an intense atmospheric water-vapor absorption band. Solar radiation at 1375 nm is virtually 100% absorbed in the troposphere before reaching the ground. In both cloudy and clear Sentinel-2 scenes, surface reflectance is near zero (10–25 DN). The resulting ultra-low MAE is physically authentic and reflects atmospheric absorption physics, not data fabrication.
